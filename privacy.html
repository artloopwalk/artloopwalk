Privacy Policy

Art Loop Walk · Last updated October 15, 2026

Art Loop Walk (the “App”) is a guide to New York City art galleries, published by [DEVELOPER NAME] (“we”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the App.

Summary

We don't collect, store, or sell personal data. The App has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. We don't run servers that receive information about you.

What stays on your device

Galleries you star as Favorites, and any walking loops you build, are saved only in the App's storage on your phone. We can't see them. They're deleted if you delete the App.

Third-party services the App contacts

To show current listings and the map, your phone connects directly to these services. Like any internet request, this shares standard technical data such as your IP address with them. We don't receive or log it.

  • GitHub: the App downloads the latest gallery list from GitHub. GitHub privacy statement
  • OpenStreetMap: map images. OpenStreetMap privacy policy
  • Google Fonts: typefaces used in the App. Google privacy policy
  • Google Maps: only when you tap to open directions, which hands the selected galleries to Google Maps. Google privacy policy

Location

The App doesn't access your device's location.

Children

The App isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.
