## Version 1.0 page

**Promotional text (170 characters max)**
This week's openings across Chelsea, Tribeca, the Lower East Side, Brooklyn and beyond, with what's on view at 250 New York galleries.

**Description**
Art Loop Walk is a walking guide to New York City's art galleries.

Browse 250 galleries by neighborhood, from Chelsea and Tribeca to the Lower East Side, the Upper East Side, Harlem, Brooklyn and Queens. Each gallery shows what's on view now and when the next opening reception is, so you can plan a night of openings or a slow Saturday walk.

• See every gallery on a map, grouped by neighborhood
• Check current exhibitions and upcoming openings at a glance
• Star galleries to keep a list of Favorites
• Build your own loop and open walking directions in Google Maps
• Listings are updated regularly and load fresh each time you open the app

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your Favorites stay on your phone.

## App Privacy

Choose **"No, we do not collect data from this app."** The label shows **Data Not Collected**.
The app has no accounts, analytics or ads, and Favorites are stored only on the device.

## Notes for App Review

Art Loop Walk is a curated guide to NYC art galleries. It works without signing in.
Open any neighborhood to see galleries with current exhibitions and openings, tap the star to save Favorites (Favorites tab), and use "Make your own loop" to build a walking route that opens in Google Maps.
Listings are refreshed from our published data so exhibition information stays current.
